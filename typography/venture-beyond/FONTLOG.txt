FONTLOG for Venture Beyond
==========================

Venture Beyond is a RaidGuild derivative of Archivo by Omnibus-Type (Hector Gatti),
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see LICENSE/OFL.txt).

Basic font information
----------------------
Family: Venture Beyond. Styles: Upright, Italic (Archivo's designs, outlines unchanged) and
Retalic (new reverse-slanted style generated from Archivo's upright).

Upstream: https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo
          commit 211127690e8ff106c36c935f7e5e697114cff103, Archivo 2.001

ChangeLog
---------
2026-11-18 (RaidGuild) Venture Beyond 0.200 variable family
  - Three variable fonts (wght 100-900, wdth 62-125; Archivo's axes, defaults and avar):
    Upright and Italic are Archivo's variable fonts renamed, with outlines, gvar, HVAR, GSUB, GPOS
    and GDEF unchanged; Retalic is Archivo's upright sheared 10 degrees left about y = 263 across
    the full design space (outlines, gvar deltas solved master-exactly, composite offsets, GPOS
    anchors and their variation deltas). Advances and kerning unchanged.
  - 54 named instances per file (6 widths x 9 weights); width 125 named "Extended".
  - usWinAscent/usWinDescent 1107/440 (family-wide extremes); line spacing unchanged.
  - v0.100 Extended Black prototype retained as the approved, hash-pinned visual baseline.

2026-11-18 (RaidGuild) Venture Beyond 0.100 prototype
  - Extended Black (wght 900, wdth 125) instances of Archivo Upright and Italic, renamed.
  - New Retalic style: Archivo upright outlines, component offsets and GPOS mark anchors sheared
    10 degrees to the left about y = 263 (half x-height), mirroring how Archivo Italic was
    slanted. Advance widths, kerning and OpenType features unchanged.
  - rvrn heavy-weight alternates baked into base glyphs (as fontmake does for statics).
  - usWinAscent/usWinDescent raised to 1107/440 so accents don't clip at Black Expanded.
    Line-spacing metrics unchanged.
  - Overlaps removed; fonts unhinted; vendor ID NONE; STAT added.
