# RaidGuild Brand Architecture

RaidGuild is the center of gravity: a builder-owned collective, shared talent pool, community, reputation, and home for ambitious applied-edge work. Specialized practices—called **spears** inside the Guild—carry focused capabilities into the market.

## Shared Foundation

- **Community:** The builder pool is the soul and renewable source of practices. It holds the people, relationships, knowledge, experiments, and shared reputation.
- **Operations:** RaidGuild LLC is the shared legal and operating entity, registered through MiDAO. Contracts, treasury, and delivery workflows can run through the LLC.
- **Market:** Practices are specialized, independently led fronts. They may own their positioning, specialization, and visual expression while drawing on the Guild's builder pool and operating layer.

The Guild is not limited to today's practices. AI and Web3 are current expressions; robotics, games, biohacking, and other fields may become practices when builders develop the expertise, ownership, and demand to lead them.

## Reigns and Practices Are Different Axes

| Axis | Question | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steward reign | **When?** | Versions the Guild identity over time and preserves historical visual and verbal systems. |
| Practice identity | **Where and for what?** | Differentiates a current area of expertise. Multiple practices can coexist within one reign. |

A practice does not become a reign because it looks different. A new reign does not require every practice to discard a useful identity. Historical change and present-day specialization can eventually be surfaced together, but the guide should keep these controls conceptually separate.

## Endorsement Standard

Distinct branding is a feature when the provenance is clear. Every endorsed practice should:

1. Use the recognizable crossed-swords mark or an approved practice variant.
2. State the relationship in plain language: **A RaidGuild practice.**
3. Make the shared operating layer legible where trust matters: **Operating through RaidGuild LLC.**
4. Link back to RaidGuild.org and provide a clear route into the shared network or intake.
5. Avoid implying that its specialty or visual system represents the entire Guild.

This is a minimum viable architecture, not a mandate for a global rebrand. It allows independently owned practices to remain distinctive while making the network, endorsement, and operational continuity visible.
